Why use this platform?

Preparing for BCA Finals is challenging. Our system bridges the gap between study and success by providing Evidence-Based Evaluation.

Using Numpy-powered Linear Regression, the system compares your current performance with previous attempts to calculate a 'Learning Slope'. It specifically detects units where your accuracy is below 50% and suggests focused revision for the TU Syllabus.
